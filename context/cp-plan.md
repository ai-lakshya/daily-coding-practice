## Why (the diagnosis)
1. **Root cause — idea-finding.** Div 2 B/C turns on one observation, not
   a known algorithm. Practice came from topic-tagged lists, which trains
   execution, not identification; when stuck, the habit was rereading or
   cycling through techniques instead of working examples.
2. **Low reps.** 1.5–3 h per problem, mostly stuck, ~1 solve/day.
3. **Passive upsolving.** Gave up in <30 min; AI wrote solution and
   write-up; never re-implemented; upsolved far above level (ABC E–G).
4. **Over-verifying in contest.** Sat on correct ideas.
5. **Fragile streaks.** 2023 and 2024 streaks died on no visible progress
   plus job/college/life.

Not the problem: contest frequency, implementation, knowledge.

## Weekly shape (~12–14 h)
- **Weekdays:** 90 min, 2 problems.
- **Weekend / contest days:** 1–2 rated rounds (ABC Saturday + CF when
  scheduled), upsolved the same day.
- **Minimum day (bad weeks):** 1 problem, 30 min. Never zero.
- **Game dev:** 2 h/week, fixed days. Everything else paused (below).

## Problem source
Codeforces problemset, rating 1100–1400, **tags hidden** (CF settings),
recent rounds. Start at 1200; move up 100 once 70% of the last 20 are
solved unaided. Once a statement is opened, it counts — no skipping to a
comfortable one.

## Stuck protocol
| time | do |
|------|----|
| 0–20 min | No rereading. Hand-work n = 1..5, or write a brute force and print its outputs. |
| 20–45 min | Prove or break the conjecture. |
| 45 min | ONE hint: editorial's first line, or an AI hint (never code). 15 more min solo. |
| 60 min | Full editorial → close it → code from memory. |
| after | 2-line note below: "observation was X; I missed it because Y". |

Anything that needed a hint or the editorial gets re-solved from scratch,
without notes, 3–7 days later.

## Contest rules
- Div 3: read A–D in the first 5 minutes before committing.
- Samples pass → at most 2 min of edge cases (n=1, all equal, overflow) →
  submit. A WA costs ~50 points; ten minutes of doubt on a B costs more.
- Upsolve only one problem past your last solve.

## Paused until CF ≥ 1200

`coding-questions` upsolves stay capped at one problem past your last solve.
